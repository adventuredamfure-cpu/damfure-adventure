Kitap Singh Tamang

Chairman / Managing Director

Overall Leadership

With more than 30 years of experience in mountaineering and Himalayan adventure, Kitap Singh Tamang brings decades of high-altitude experience and leadership to Damfure Adventure. His career is built on extensive exploration of the world's highest mountains and a deep understanding of Himalayan expedition operations.

He has stood on the summit of Mount Everest five times and has completed major expeditions across the Himalayas, including Manaslu, Cho Oyu, Shishapangma, Lhotse and Annapurna.

Mountaineering Achievements

  • Manaslu: Summited 5 times
  • 8000m-class mountains: Cho Oyu, Shishapangma, Lhotse and Annapurna
  • 7000m+ peaks: Janak Chuli, Ratna Chuli and Ama Dablam
  • Janak Chuli: First summiter
  • Great Himalayan Trail: Completed in 2019
  • High-altitude experience: More than 56 climbs above 6,000m

As Chairman and Managing Director, Kitap Singh Tamang provides overall leadership and strategic direction for Damfure Adventure, helping guide the company toward responsible, professional and experience-driven adventure tourism in Nepal.
