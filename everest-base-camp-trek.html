THE JOURNEY

From Kathmandu to the foot of Everest.

A carefully paced Himalayan journey designed around acclimatization, mountain scenery, local culture and time in the Khumbu. We welcome solo trekkers and groups from 1 to 15 people.

14-DAY ITINERARY
Day 01 • Kathmandu

Arrival in Kathmandu

Arrive in Kathmandu, meet the Damfure Adventure team and transfer to your hotel. Receive a trek briefing and prepare your equipment for the journey.

Altitude: ~1,400 m
Day 02 • Kathmandu

Kathmandu Sightseeing & Trek Preparation

Explore Kathmandu and its cultural heritage sites, followed by final trek preparation, equipment checks and briefing with your guide.

Overnight: Kathmandu
Day 03 • Lukla → Phakding

Fly to Lukla and Trek to Phakding

Take the scenic mountain flight to Lukla and begin your trek through the Dudh Koshi valley toward Phakding.

Altitude: Lukla ~2,840 m • Phakding ~2,610 m
Trekking: 3–4 hrs
Day 04 • Phakding → Namche Bazaar

Trek to Namche Bazaar

Cross suspension bridges and follow the Dudh Koshi valley toward the famous Sherpa trading town of Namche Bazaar.

Altitude: ~3,440 m
Trekking: 5–6 hrs
Day 05 • Namche Bazaar

Acclimatization Day at Namche Bazaar

Take a gradual acclimatization hike toward Everest View Hotel and enjoy panoramic views of Everest, Ama Dablam, Lhotse and the surrounding peaks.

Hiking: 3–4 hrs
Overnight: Namche Bazaar
Day 06 • Namche → Tengboche

Trek to Tengboche

Walk through forests and open mountain viewpoints toward Tengboche, home to the famous Tengboche Monastery, with spectacular views of Ama Dablam and surrounding peaks.

Altitude: ~3,860 m
Trekking: 5–6 hrs
Day 07 • Tengboche → Dingboche

Trek to Dingboche

Continue through Pangboche and the Imja Valley toward Dingboche as the landscape becomes increasingly alpine.

Altitude: ~4,410 m
Trekking: 5–6 hrs
Day 08 • Dingboche

Acclimatization Day at Dingboche

Take a gradual acclimatization hike toward Nagarjun Hill and enjoy views of the surrounding Himalayan peaks before returning to Dingboche.

Hiking: 3–4 hrs
Overnight: Dingboche
Day 09 • Dingboche → Lobuche

Trek to Lobuche

Continue through the high Himalayan landscape toward Lobuche, following the valley closer to the Khumbu Glacier.

Altitude: ~4,910 m
Trekking: 5–6 hrs
Day 10 • Lobuche → Gorak Shep → Everest Base Camp

Trek to Everest Base Camp

Walk to Gorak Shep and continue across the rugged high-altitude terrain to Everest Base Camp. After spending time at Base Camp, return to Gorak Shep.

Everest Base Camp: ~5,364 m
Gorak Shep: ~5,140 m
Trekking: 7–8 hrs
Day 11 • Kala Patthar → Pheriche

Hike to Kala Patthar and Trek to Pheriche

Start early for the hike to Kala Patthar, one of the classic viewpoints for Everest and the surrounding Himalayan peaks. Descend afterward toward Pheriche.

Kala Patthar: ~5,545 m
Pheriche: ~4,240 m
Trekking: 7–8 hrs
Day 12 • Pheriche → Namche Bazaar

Trek to Namche Bazaar

Descend through the Khumbu Valley, passing Pangboche and Tengboche before continuing toward Namche Bazaar.

Destination: Namche Bazaar
Trekking: 6–7 hrs
Day 13 • Namche → Lukla

Trek Back to Lukla

Follow the Dudh Koshi valley back toward Lukla and complete the trekking section of your Everest Base Camp journey.

Altitude: Lukla ~2,840 m
Trekking: 6–7 hrs
Day 14 • Lukla → Kathmandu

Fly Back to Kathmandu / Departure

Take the mountain flight from Lukla back to Kathmandu. Airport transfer and final departure arrangements can be organized according to your international flight schedule.

Flight: ~35 minutes, subject to weather and operational conditions
